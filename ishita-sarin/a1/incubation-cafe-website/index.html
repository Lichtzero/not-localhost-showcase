The premise

Why a café, and why incubation.

Incubation Café is a 10-week module inside the second-year MDes studio: a running bench where ideas get poured out early, tasted while they're rough, and sent back for another round. It sits between a brief and a finished thesis project — the part usually kept private.

This site is that bench made public. Fifteen designers, fifteen concurrent projects, each logged at whatever stage it happens to be in — grounds, brewing, steeping, or freshly poured. Nothing here is final, and that's the point: it's a record of work being made, not work that's done.
